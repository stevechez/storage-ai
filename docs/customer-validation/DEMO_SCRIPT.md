# Demo Script — Sprint 17

Grounded in the actual `/dashboard` and the real seed data on it (facility: "Lonestar Self Storage"). Follow along on the live dashboard while you talk — don't just describe it.

Target length: 5 minutes. Don't narrate every screen — the acceptance bar for this product is that it explains itself.

---

## 1. Problem (30 seconds)

Say it plainly, don't slide-deck it:

> "Storage facilities lose rental opportunities when calls happen after hours, or when whoever's on the phone is already helping a walk-in. The renter just calls the next facility on the list. You never even know you lost them."

Pause. Let them agree or push back — their answer here is the most valuable thing you'll hear all conversation.

## 2. Solution, one sentence

> "IntelliLease answers that call, figures out what the renter needs, and turns it into something you can act on in the morning — without you having to listen to every recording."

## 3. Workflow — walk the dashboard top to bottom

**Point at the call, not the AI.** Open with the after-hours example already in the demo data:

> "This one came in at 9pm — a customer wanted a 10x15 unit, needed it ASAP. Nobody was at the desk. Here's what IntelliLease did with it."

Walk through, in order:

1. **Good Morning** — "This is what you'd see opening the dashboard the next morning: how many opportunities need attention today, how many are waiting on follow-up, what converted recently. Five seconds, not five minutes of listening to voicemails."
2. **Today's Actions** — "Here's that 9pm call. It knows the unit size, the urgency, and it's telling you — not asking you — to call this person back immediately, before someone else does."
3. **Active Opportunities** — "Every opportunity, current status — needs follow-up, contacted, converted, or lost — right on the card. And a suggested response you can copy and send yourself. You're always the one who hits send — this doesn't call or text anyone automatically."
4. **Recent Results** — "What actually happened in the last day: how many came in, how many converted, how many are still open."
5. **Revenue Impact** — "And the number that actually matters: here's the estimated monthly revenue these opportunities represent, and what's already been captured from the ones that converted. This is an estimate off an assumed rate, not your real billing — it's here to make the value concrete, not to replace your books."

## 4. Business value — land the plane

> "The point isn't the AI. The point is that call at 9pm didn't just disappear. It became a number in a report you'd actually look at."

Then stop talking. Ask the first discovery question (see `DISCOVERY_QUESTIONS.md`) and listen.

---

## What NOT to do

- Don't lead with "AI" or explain the technology. If asked how it works, one sentence: "it reads the call for intent, priority, and next step — deterministic rules today, not a black box." Move on.
- Don't apologize for it being a demo with sample data — the demo banner already says so. Just use it.
- Don't demo every button. If they don't ask about "Mark Converted" or "Copy Response," don't explain them.
