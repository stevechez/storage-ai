# Operator Feedback Log

One entry per conversation. Capture what they actually said, in their words where possible — not your interpretation of it. A conversation that contradicts the product's assumptions is more valuable than one that confirms them.

Copy the template below for each new conversation.

---

## [Facility name] — [Date]

**Facility type:** (independent / family-owned / small regional chain / other)
**Approximate size:** (unit count or rough size, if they shared it)

**Current call process:**
(How do they actually handle inbound calls today — who answers, what happens after hours, any voicemail/answering service in place)

**Pain points:**
(In their words — what's actually frustrating, not what you expected to hear)

**Existing tools:**
(PMS or other software already in use, and what it does/doesn't do with a call once it comes in)

**Strong reactions:**
(Anything they responded to noticeably — positively or negatively — during the demo or conversation)

**Objections:**
(Reasons they gave for hesitation, doubt, or "not for me" — record verbatim if possible)

**Requested features:**
(Anything they specifically asked for — note whether it's Customer Requested, Revenue Enabling, or Trust Enabling per the Feature Decision Framework)

**Buying signal:**
(Did they indicate they'd actually pay for this, and under what condition? Or did they not get there?)
