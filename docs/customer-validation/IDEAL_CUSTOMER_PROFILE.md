# Ideal Customer Profile — Sprint 17

## Target

- Independent self-storage owners (not part of a national brand)
- Family-owned facilities, often owner-operated or run by a small staff
- Small regional operators — typically 1–5 facilities, not a portfolio
- Facilities without a call center or after-hours answering service
- Likely already using a basic PMS (SiteLink, StorEdge, Storable) for unit/tenant management, but with no automated phone handling layered on top

## Why this profile

These operators feel the core problem StorageAI solves most acutely:

- The owner or a single employee is answering calls between running the office, walking the lot, and handling walk-ins — after-hours and during-hours calls both get missed.
- They don't have budget or appetite for enterprise call-center software, but they do feel the lost-rental pain directly and personally (it's their own revenue, not a line item in a regional P&L).
- They can make a buying decision themselves, without procurement or a multi-stakeholder sales cycle.

## Avoid initially

- Large REIT operators (Public Storage, Extra Space, CubeSmart, etc.) — enterprise procurement cycles, existing call-center contracts, PMS integrations required before a demo is even relevant
- Enterprise chains with centralized call handling
- Facilities with complex, custom PMS environments where "connect to our system" would be a prerequisite before value can be shown

## How to find them

- Google Maps search for "self storage" in a target metro/region — filter for facilities with a small number of reviews and no obvious corporate branding
- Local Facebook groups / Nextdoor for small business owners
- Self Storage Association (SSA) local chapter directories
- Storage auction / classifieds sites often list independent facility contacts
- Ask the first operator you talk to for a referral to another independent owner — this audience trusts peer recommendations more than cold outreach
