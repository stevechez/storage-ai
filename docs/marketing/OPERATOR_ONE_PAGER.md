# IntelliLease — for Independent Storage Operators

*One page. Leave-behind after a founder demo. No AI hype — see `docs/demo/FOUNDER_DEMO_SCRIPT.md` for why.*

## The problem

A renter calls at 9pm. It rings. Nobody answers. They call the next facility.

You never see that call as a loss — it just never shows up as anything. No missed-call badge on a revenue report. No unit rented. It's the rental you didn't know you lost, and it happens every week the phone isn't answered by someone who can say yes.

## The solution

IntelliLease answers immediately, figures out what the renter actually needs, and gives your team a clear next action — so an after-hours call becomes a lead you can follow up on instead of a call that just disappeared.

It is not a chatbot bolted onto your website, and it doesn't try to replace you. It's a digital leasing employee: it listens, it understands, it hands you exactly what you need to close the rental yourself.

## What it does today

- **Answers your phone** — a real conversation, not a menu of button-press options
- **Captures what the renter needs** — unit size, timing, what they asked about
- **Produces a transcript** for every call, automatically
- **Reads the conversation** — flags what it is (a rental, a pricing question, an availability check) and how urgent it is
- **Gives you a recommended next step** for every call, ranked by priority
- **Never invents a price or confirms availability it doesn't actually have** — it defers to you and captures a callback number instead. This is deliberate: it's a filter and a first responder, not a replacement for your judgment on pricing and inventory.
- **One dashboard** showing every call, every opportunity, and what it's worth — updated automatically, no data entry

## What it's not (yet)

It doesn't talk to your PMS, send SMS, process payments, or manage multiple locations. Those aren't oversights — they're deliberately not built until real pilot facilities show they're actually needed. Building speculative features nobody's asked for yet is exactly what this product avoids.

## The pilot offer

A founder-supported pilot, not a "sign up and figure it out yourself" trial. You get direct access to the person building it, real involvement while you use it on real calls, and no obligation to continue if it doesn't show real value in the first month.
